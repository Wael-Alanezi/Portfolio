## What we are building

A near-infrared camera mounted on a drone measures vegetation health across the university garden using NDVI. The output is a map that shows where plants are healthy and where they need attention.

## Scope

A one to two semester proof of concept on a minimal budget, built by a team of five students at the Riyadh Space Lighthouse, Prince Sultan University.

## Status

In progress with my team. Results will come from the drone survey.
